Privacy Policy

At **IOIS (Indian Online Income Supporting System)**, accessible from our official website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and members.

1. Information We Collect

When you register for our ₹10 Digital Starter Kit, we collect your Full Name, Email Address, WhatsApp Mobile Number, Address, and UPI Payment details. Mobile numbers are kept hidden/masked on public ID badges for member security.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used to process digital kit access via Google Drive, process referral rewards (70% bonus payouts), and generate your official IOIS Digital ID Card.

3. Data Security

We do not sell or share user personal data with third-party advertisers. All transaction details remain secure between the user and IOIS Administration.
